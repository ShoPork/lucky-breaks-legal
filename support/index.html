Frequently asked questions

How is my progress saved? Lucky Breaks saves your board, piece tray, score, high score, coins and shop upgrades on your device after every move. Close the app any time and tap Continue on the home screen to pick up where you left off.
Do I need an internet connection? No. You can play the whole game offline, with no account. The internet is used only for the online leaderboard and for ads.
How does the leaderboard work? The leaderboard uses Game Center on iPhone and iPad, and Google Play Games on Android. Sign in to Game Center or Google Play Games and your high score is posted automatically, under your nickname or gamer name. Signing in is optional.
My name or rank doesn't show on the leaderboard (Android). If your Google Play Games profile is private, your score is saved but other players can't see your name or rank. You can change this in the Google Play Games app under your profile settings.
How do I turn off sound or music? Tap the gear icon (Settings) on the home screen or during a game to turn sound effects, music and haptics on or off, change the volume, or switch the board theme.
How do I reset my game data? Open Settings → Delete All Data in the app. This clears your saved run, high score, coins and upgrades on your device. Scores already posted to the leaderboard are kept by Game Center or Google Play Games.
