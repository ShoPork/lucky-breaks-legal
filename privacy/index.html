Privacy Policy — Lucky Breaks

Effective date: November 13, 2026  |  Developer: Shoham Bracha

1. Overview

Lucky Breaks is a single-player puzzle game for iOS and Android. You don't need an account with us to play, and we don't run any servers of our own. We don't ask for your name, email address or any other personal information inside the game.

This policy explains what stays on your device, what is shared with Apple or Google when you use the online leaderboard, and what the advertising service collects.

2. Game data stored on your device

Your game progress is stored only on your device: the current board, piece tray, score, high score, coins, shop upgrades, and settings such as sound, music, haptics and theme. We never upload this data to any server.

You can erase it at any time in the app under Settings → Delete All Data. Uninstalling the app also removes it.

3. Online leaderboard (Game Center and Google Play Games)

Lucky Breaks has an optional online leaderboard. It is provided by Apple Game Center on iOS and Google Play Games Services on Android. The game runs the leaderboard through these services; we don't receive or store your leaderboard data ourselves.

If you are signed in to Game Center or Google Play Games:

  • Your score is submitted: the game sends your high score to the platform's leaderboard.
  • Other players can see it: your score appears on the leaderboard together with your Game Center nickname or Play Games gamer name, and your rank.
  • You see other players: the game downloads the top scores, with those players' public names, and your own rank, to show the leaderboard screen.

Signing in is optional. If you aren't signed in, nothing is sent to the leaderboard and you can still play the full game.

Apple and Google handle this data under their own privacy policies:

  • Apple Privacy Policy (Game Center)
  • Google Privacy Policy (Google Play Games)

Note: Delete All Data in the app only clears data on your device. It doesn't remove scores already on the leaderboard. To control your public profile or remove your scores, use your Game Center settings on iOS, or the Google Play Games app or your Google Account settings on Android. On Android, if your Play Games profile is private, your score is still recorded but other players can't see your name or rank.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Lucky Breaks is free and shows ads from Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK):

  • a banner ad on the game screen,
  • an ad at game over,
  • an optional rewarded video if you choose to revive a run.

The game asks for non-personalised ads for the game-over and rewarded ads. To deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and limit how often you see an ad, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • Identifiers: your device's advertising ID (Google Advertising ID on Android; on iOS, Apple's IDFA only if you allow tracking) and IP address
  • Approximate location: derived from your IP address
  • Usage data: ads shown and your interactions with them
  • Diagnostics: crash and performance data related to showing ads

Learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

5. Internet use

You can play Lucky Breaks offline. An internet connection is used only for the leaderboard and for ads.

6. Children's privacy

Lucky Breaks is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. We don't collect personal information ourselves; game progress stays on the device.

7. Your choices

  • Delete game data: Settings → Delete All Data in the app.
  • Leaderboard: don't sign in to Game Center or Google Play Games, or manage your profile and scores in those services.
  • Ads (iOS): Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.
  • Ads (Android): Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) to reset or delete your advertising ID.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
